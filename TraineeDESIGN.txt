1. PRIMARY DESIGN GOAL

Transform the current trainee portal into:

A personalized learning command center that clearly tells the trainee what to learn, why it matters, what needs attention, and what to do next.

When a trainee opens the platform, within 5 seconds they should understand:

What am I currently learning?

What should I do next?

How much progress have I made?

Which competencies are strong?

Which competencies need improvement?

Do I have any assessments pending?

What should I revise?

What courses/resources are recommended?

Who is my assigned trainer?

What achievements have I earned?

The interface should reduce cognitive load.

Do not make the trainee search through multiple cards to figure out their next action.
