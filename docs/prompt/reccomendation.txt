============================================================

32. DIVERSITY RE-RANKING
============================================================

Do not force diversity when there is a critical skill gap that legitimately requires several related courses.

Use a configurable MMR-like approach.

Conceptually:

RerankScore =
    lambda * relevance
    -
    (1-lambda) * similarityToSelected
