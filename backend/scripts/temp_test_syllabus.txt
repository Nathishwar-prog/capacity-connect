COURSE OVERVIEW
This comprehensive course trains severe weather forecasters on jet stream coupling and Doppler radar mesocyclone detection.

TARGET AUDIENCE
Operational Aeronautical and Severe Weather Forecasters.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
• Analyze upper-level jet streaks and ageostrophic circulations.
• Detect mesocyclones and tornado vortex signatures (TVS) on Doppler velocity products.

MODULE 1: Synoptic Weather Systems
Covers jet streak dynamics and upper tropospheric divergence.

1.1 Frontal Dynamics & Jet Streams
OBJECTIVES
• Identify jet streak quadrants associated with ascent.
• Evaluate quasi-geostrophic vertical velocity.

Jet streaks induce four-quadrant ageostrophic circulation patterns with divergence in the right entrance and left exit regions.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
• Right entrance and left exit quadrants favor upward vertical motion.

KNOWLEDGE CHECK
Question 1: In a straight jet streak, which quadrant exhibits upper-level divergence?
A) Left entrance
B) Right entrance *(Correct)*
C) Right exit
D) Center axis
Answer: B
Explanation: Mass divergence occurs in the right entrance and left exit quadrants due to sub-geostrophic deceleration.

MODULE 2: Doppler Radar Interpretation
Covers convective storm morphology and radar moments.

2.1 Supercell Signatures & Hook Echo Detection
OBJECTIVES
• Identify hook echo and bounded weak echo regions (BWER).
• Calculate rotational velocity in mesocyclones.

Supercell thunderstorms exhibit persistent rotating updrafts identifiable by velocity couplets and cyclonic shear.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
• Hook echoes indicate precipitation wrapped around the rotating updraft.

KNOWLEDGE CHECK
Question 2: A bounded weak echo region (BWER) in a supercell thunderstorm is created by:
A) Strong downdraft evaporating hydrometeors
B) Intense updraft suspending precipitation aloft *(Correct)*
C) Complete radar beam attenuation
D) Ground clutter interference
Answer: B
Explanation: Strong updrafts prevent large precipitation particles from descending, leaving a low-reflectivity core surrounded by echo aloft.

GLOSSARY
BWER: Bounded Weak Echo Region.
Mesocyclone: A cyclonically rotating vortex in a convective storm.
